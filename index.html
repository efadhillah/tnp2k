TNP2K · Timeline of Succession & Reform 2010–2025

Institutional evolution of Indonesia’s poverty reduction coordination framework and the consumption-decile targeting system, based on official regulations, program documents, international impact evaluations, and media coverage.
2004–2011 Legal foundation for social security UU 40/2004 · UU 24/2011
UU No. 40/2004 (SJSN) established the national social security system. UU No. 24/2011 (BPJS) created BPJS Kesehatan and BPJS Ketenagakerjaan to implement SJSN. These laws provided the legal umbrella for the health insurance expansion (KIS/PBI) that TNP2K’s targeting data would later serve.
Official documents: UU 40/2004 (SJSN); UU 24/2011 (BPJS).
2010 Formulation & Launch Perpres 15/2010
TNP2K established by Presidential Regulation No. 15/2010, chaired by the Vice President, to coordinate poverty reduction across ministries. It replaced the previous coordination body (Perpres 13/2009). DFAT’s PRSF launched in April 2010.
Official analysis (DFAT ICR, 2015): PRSF was “a creature of circumstances” — a combination of “the election of a reform orientated Vice President with an academic background” and Australia’s willingness to take risks. The approach “relied heavily on the Indonesian leadership to drive an agenda in a highly contested policy space” [citation:1].
Official document: Perpres 15/2010.
2011–2012 BDT built & first programs targeted PPLS 2011 · BSM · Raskin · Jamkesmas
The Unified Database (BDT) was developed from PPLS 2011 data using a district-specific Proxy Means Test (PMT) anchored to BPS Susenas. The BDT classified households by consumption decile (desil), enabling precise targeting. By 2012, the BDT was used for Raskin, PKH, BSM, and Jamkesmas.
International evidence (3ie/J-PAL, 2012): A randomized controlled trial across 600–640 villages compared PMT, community targeting, and self-targeting. The study found that self-targeting identified poorer beneficiaries at lower administrative cost, while community targeting achieved higher satisfaction but was less precise at identifying the poor [citation:4][citation:5]. This evaluation directly informed TNP2K’s discussions on updating the Unified Database [citation:13].
Reference: TNP2K SOP BDT; 3ie Impact Evaluation Report 12.
2013 BLSM & KPS — crisis compensation via BDT BLSM · KPS
When fuel subsidies were cut, the government used the BDT to deliver Bantuan Langsung Sementara Masyarakat (BLSM) — unconditional cash transfers to 15.5 million households. The Kartu Perlindungan Sosial (KPS) was introduced as the first national beneficiary card.
Reference: ANTARA — BLSM Rp9.32T approved.
2014 KKS · KIS · KIP — integrated card-based reform P4S · JKN
The BDT deciles powered the launch of three flagship programs on 3 November 2014:
  • KKS (Kartu Keluarga Sejahtera) — replaced KPS, linked to Simpanan Keluarga Sejahtera.
  • KIS (Kartu Indonesia Sehat) — health insurance for 86.4 million PBI under JKN/BPJS, replacing Jamkesmas.
  • KIP (Kartu Indonesia Pintar) — education cash transfers for children aged 7–18 from poor families, replacing and expanding BSM.
Reference: Kemenko PMK — Launch of KKS, KIP, KIS.
2015 Perpres 96/2015 — expanded mandate MAHKOTA · DTPPFM
Perpres No. 96/2015 amended the 2010 regulation, expanding TNP2K’s membership. PRSF concluded and was replaced by MAHKOTA Indonesia (2015–2023). In 2016, at the request of the Social Minister, TNP2K handed over management of the BDT to Kemensos (renamed DTPPFM).
Official analysis (DFAT ICR, 2015): The Quality Assurance system “was not universally welcomed by all stakeholders.” Many at TNP2K saw it as “a management control or compliance tool being imposed on them.” Strong Embassy engagement made the difference in establishing it [citation:1].
Official documents: Perpres 96/2015.
2017–2021 Data fragmentation & P3KE workaround DTKS · Regsosek · P3KE
The BDT formally became DTKS under Kemensos regulations. TNP2K retained updating functions with BPS. In 2021, TNP2K worked with BKKBN on P3KE — a by-name-by-address extreme poverty targeting dataset.
Official analysis (ODI, 2015): PRSF “found it hard to access the decisions being made about the work.” TNP2K “had little or no incentives to keep PRSF informed.” The programme “found itself report to one government and support another, with very little authority or influence on either one” [citation:9].
International evidence (NBER, 2025): A study of community targeting at scale during COVID-19 (8 million households) found “strongly progressive targeting” but “not quite as strong as if they had used a fully up-to-date proxy means test” [citation:6][citation:14].
2022 Extreme Poverty Push — Inpres 4/2022 Multi-ministry mandate
Inpres No. 4/2022 launched the Extreme Poverty Elimination Acceleration program, with three strategic pillars: (1) reducing expenditure burden, (2) increasing income/productivity, (3) reducing poverty pockets. P3KE became the operational data instrument for by-name, by-address, by-NIK targeting.
Official analysis (World Bank IEG, 2026): The Bank Group’s support for human capital (FY13–FY23) followed a “deliberate sequence: early diagnostics identified binding constraints, sustained analytics deepened policy dialogue, and scaled-up financing followed once reform momentum and institutional readiness strengthened.” More than half of advisory services “directly informed lending,” and 43% “contributed to national strategies” [citation:2].
Media coverage (Tempo, Jan 2024): INDEF assessed the 0% target as “difficult to achieve,” citing DTKS updating challenges and new entrants into extreme poverty. → Read full Tempo article
Official document: Inpres No. 4/2022.
2023 PROAKTIF launches as successor to MAHKOTA PROAKTIF (2023–2025)
PROAKTIF (Poverty Alleviation and Comprehensive, Inclusive and Adaptive Social Protection) replaced MAHKOTA as DFAT’s support facility for TNP2K’s Policy Team. This marked 15 years of continuous Australian support (2010–2025).
Media coverage (Tempo, Aug 2023): Celios noted the 0% extreme poverty target was a “big challenge” because extreme poor populations are in remote villages. → Read full Tempo article
Reference: DT Global — PROAKTIF program page.
2024 Succession — TNP2K to BP Taskin Perpres 163/2024
TNP2K was formally succeeded on 5 November 2024 by Presidential Regulation No. 163/2024, which created BP Taskin (Badan Percepatan Pengentasan Kemiskinan) as a non-structural body reporting to the President. The Perpres explicitly revoked both Perpres 15/2010 and Perpres 96/2015.
Media coverage (Tempo, Jan 2024): INDEF’s Tauhid Ahmad stated the 0% target was “somewhat difficult to achieve,” citing inadequate DTKS updating. → Read full Tempo article
Official document: Perpres 163/2024.
2025 DTSEN consolidation & PROAKTIF conclusion Inpres 4/2025
The three datasets — DTKS, Regsosek, P3KE — were merged into DTSEN (Data Tunggal Sosial Ekonomi Nasional) under Presidential Instruction No. 4/2025, signed 5 February 2025. The Inpres instructs for optimalisasi integrasi data sosial dan ekonomi nasional with akurasi, interoperabilitas, dan pemutakhiran data. PROAKTIF concluded in 2025, ending 15 years of DFAT-supported TNP2K operations.
Official analysis (OECD, 2019/2024): Indonesia’s social protection reform faces a “kapasitas kelembagaan” challenge: “Fragmentasi tata kelola, keterbatasan integrasi data, dan koordinasi antar lembaga yang belum optimal” are more determining factors than normative obstacles. Comparative analysis of South Korea, Chile, and Vietnam shows “no single social protection reform model” — success depends on each country’s ability to adapt policy to its own institutional conditions [citation:3].
Media coverage (Kompas, Feb 2025): Populi Center noted DTSEN’s main challenge is “kurangnya sinergi antarinstansi” (lack of inter-agency synergy). → Read full Kompas article
Official document: Inpres No. 4/2025.

📋 Institutional succession

TNP2K’s coordination mandate moved to BP Taskin (Perpres 163/2024).

🗂️ Data consolidation

1 registry

DTKS, Regsosek, and P3KE merged into DTSEN (Inpres 4/2025).

🧮 Technical legacy

The Proxy Means Test (PMT) — district-specific, Susenas-anchored — survived inside BPS and generates DTSEN’s decile rankings.

📊 Targeting evidence

RCTs show PMT outperforms community targeting on precision, while community methods achieve higher satisfaction. Self-targeting is more cost-effective [citation:4][citation:5][citation:6].
